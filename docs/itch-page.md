## Description
Grab up to 3 friends, share a 6-letter room code, and pop every orb before the clock runs out.
Big orbs split into smaller ones... and that orb your friend split? The game remembers who killed you.

- 1-4 players online, each on their own phone or PC, no download
- 25 levels, special orbs, sky events, rare crates and relics
- Shove mode, end-of-match awards (Friendly Fire, First to Die...), emotes
- SAVE CLIP: keep the last seconds as a video for TikTok
- Daily Level: same level for everyone, compare scores

Controls: Arrows / A-D to move, Space / Q to fire, 1-4 emotes, C save clip.
