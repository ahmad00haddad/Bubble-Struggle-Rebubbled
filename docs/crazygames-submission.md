## Short description
Co-op orb-popping chaos for 1-4 friends. Split orbs, blame your friends, save the clip.

## Description
Grab up to 3 friends, share a 6-letter room code, and pop every orb before the clock runs out. Big orbs split into smaller ones, and that orb your friend split? The game remembers who killed you.

- 1-4 players online, each on their own phone or PC
- 25 levels, special orbs, sky events, rare crates and relics
- Shove mode, end-of-match awards, quick emotes
- Daily Level: the same level for everyone each day

## Controls
Arrows or A/D move · Space or Q fire · 1-4 emotes · Esc pause. Touch buttons on mobile.
